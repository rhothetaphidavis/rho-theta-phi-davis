01 — About

About Sigma Eta Pi

Sigma Eta Pi was founded in 2010 at UCLA. It is a co-ed professional entrepreneurship fraternity with chapters at universities across the country.

Rather than steering everyone down one track, Sigma Eta Pi supports members on whatever path they choose, from startups to careers in tech, finance and consulting.
