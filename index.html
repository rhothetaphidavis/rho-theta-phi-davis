01 — Our name

Three coordinates. Any point in space.

In spherical coordinates, every point in three-dimensional space is described by three numbers:

ρrho
the distance from the origin to the point.
θtheta
the angle around the z-axis, measured in the xy-plane from the positive x-axis.
φphi
the angle down from the positive z-axis.

Together, ρ, θ and φ can locate any point in 3D space. That is the idea behind our name: every member, whatever their major or background, has a place in ΡΘΦ.

x = ρ sin φ cos θ y = ρ sin φ sin θ z = ρ cos φ
